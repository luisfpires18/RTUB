# My Tuno — Stage Enemy Scaling Tables

**System:** Tiered enemies — all stages within a tier share the same flat stats (no per-stage formula).  
**Boss stats** are explicit overrides per tier (not a multiplier of normal stats).  
**Action time** decreases every 100 stages: 5.0s → 4.5s → ... → 1.0s min.
